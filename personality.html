Short Essay on LLM's as a tool not a crutch

The statement, "LLMs must be used as a tool, not a crutch", means that AI shouldn't be used as a replacement for our everyday brainstorming, but as a way to support our learning. A LLM's role is to help me understand difficult concepts but I, as an individual, still still responsible for learning the fundamentals, and completing my academic work.

As a student studing Computer Networking and Security. I use LLMs in several ways to improve my learning. For example, when learning Database Design, suing SQL, I asked it to explain concepts and help me identify errors in my code and explain why what I inputted couldn't be used.
Its more useful in cases such as this because instead of letting it do the entire assignment, I would do the assignment and use the knowledge learnt and apply the knowledge somewhere else.

Another method of using LLMs is through studying for exams. LLMs such as ChatGPT can be used to generate quiz questions that I can answer based on information thought by our lecturer. This method normally keeps me engaged.

A study odne by the University of South Californa states that students who are encouraged by lecturers to use AI for learning-orienated reasons, they normally have a hard time using the AI generated information to replace information they research themselves.
